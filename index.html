About Full Monty British Pub & Cider House

Full Monty British Pub & Cider House opened in December 2004, making it Japan's oldest cider house. We bring the atmosphere of a neighbourhood British pub to Yokohama. Expect a relaxed space, good conversation, and a line-up that celebrates real cider and quality beer.

We focus on small producers and interesting pours - from bone-dry farmhouse styles to juicy modern ciders - alongside carefully curated beers and perrys.

What to expect

  • Rotating taps with limited kegs
  • British-style pub snacks and hearty dishes
  • Friendly, English and Japanese-speaking staff

Food

Bangers & mash, shepherd's pie, and other pub favorites. Hearty, satisfying, and authentically British.
